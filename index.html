Privacy Policy — Neighbor Solar

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Developer: Neighbor Solar
Contact: Sushi.Myuzu@outlook.com

Neighbor Solar is a simple app that shows the live energy flow and per-panel output of your own SunPower solar system. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app collects no personal data and sends nothing to any server.

Information the app stores on your device

Everything the app needs stays in storage on your own phone and never leaves it:

  • Your solar gateway's address on your home Wi-Fi network, its model, and its serial number, so the app can reconnect to it.
  • A daily baseline of your panels' lifetime energy counters, used to work out today's per-panel production.
  • Today's production samples (solar, home, and grid power readings), used to draw today's production chart. Samples are only for today and are replaced each day.
  • Whether your subscription is active, and when that was last checked.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

How the app talks to your solar system

The app connects only to your own SunPower PVS5 or PVS6 gateway on your home Wi-Fi network, to read live power readings and panel data. Your gateway sign-in is used only on your home network, between your phone and your gateway, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Neighbor Solar is sold as a yearly subscription with a free trial. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or handle your payment details; Google handles them under Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No crash reporting.
  • No sharing of any data with any third party.

Support email

The only information we ever receive about you is what you choose to send us yourself, for example if you email us at Sushi.Myuzu@outlook.com for support. We use that only to answer you.

Children

Neighbor Solar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
